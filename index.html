Design Thinking & Innovation

Five pillars that shaped the WALL-E BIK PRO from empathy to real-world validation.

Observe the struggle of short-distance travel and the desire for “productive” exercise. Users want a sustainable way to travel that doesn’t rely solely on a wall outlet. They want to feel like they are contributing to the vehicle’s movement.

“Commuters need a zero-emission transport device that utilizes both human movement and solar energy to reduce dependency on the electrical grid.” Core requirements: convert walking to electricity, capture solar efficiently, remain safe to balance while the treadmill is moving.

Dual-input hybrid system: Kinetic path (Treadmill belt → DC Generator → Battery) + Solar path (PV Panels → Charge Controller → Battery). Safety features include hand-brake systems and speed limiters so the treadmill cannot “runaway” from the user. Diode protection prevents reverse power flow.

Chassis: modified scooter frame housing a flat-bed treadmill. Power plant: motor mounted in reverse as generator under the belt + solar film/panels. Energy management: diode system + Li-ion battery. Drive train: hub motor in the wheel. Hydraulic suspension, hidden wiring, strong steel frame.

Efficiency, solar yield, grip texture, and balance are validated in real conditions. Refinements may include larger solar surface area or increased treadmill resistance for higher kinetic generation. Continuous iteration ensures safety, comfort, and maximum hybrid yield.
